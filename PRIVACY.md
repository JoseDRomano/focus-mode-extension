# Focus Mode privacy policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

Focus Mode is a Chrome extension that blocks distracting websites during the work
hours you choose and hides distracting parts of some sites the rest of the time.

## What Focus Mode stores

- **Your settings**: work hours, blocked sites, cleanup options, break allowance and
  lock time. These are saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`, so Chrome can sync them
  between your own signed-in browsers.
- **Your usage stats**: how many times a blocked site was opened, focus minutes,
  breaks taken and your bypass history. These are saved with Chrome's
  `storage.local` and never leave your device. Daily counters are kept for 30 days;
  the bypass history keeps the last 50 entries.

## What Focus Mode does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or remote code.
- It does not read the content of the pages you visit. On YouTube, LinkedIn, X,
  Reddit and Instagram it adds styles that hide page sections you chose to hide, and
  it reads the page address to know which page you are on.

## Permissions

- **declarativeNetRequest**: redirects blocked sites to Focus Mode's "blocked" page
  during work hours.
- **Access to all websites**: Chrome only lets an extension redirect a page when it
  has access to both that page and the page the visit started from (for example a
  search result). Focus Mode uses this access only to redirect the sites on your
  block list and to hide the feed sections you chose.
- **storage**: saves the settings and stats described above.
- **alarms**: checks once a minute whether work hours have started or ended.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Focus Mode deletes everything it stored.
